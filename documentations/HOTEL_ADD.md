# Provision New Hotel — Enterprise Hotel Provisioning

## Objective

Update the **“Provision New Hotel”** page into a professional, enterprise-level hotel provisioning workflow using a **stepper form**. The form should be organized into clear sections so administrators can complete hotel onboarding progressively and review information before submission.

---

## Step 1 — Personal Details

Collect the primary owner/contact person's personal and identification information.

1. **Full Name**
2. **Signature**
3. **Phone Number 1**
4. **Phone Number 2**
5. **Email 1**
6. **Email 2**
7. **Legal Document Number**
8. **Residential Address**
9. **Business Address**
10. **Government ID Proof**

---

## Step 2 — Hotel Details

Collect the property's basic identification and branding information.

1. **Legal Property Name**
2. **Hotel Name**
3. **Property Type**

   * Guest House
   * Hotel
   * Palace
   * Stay House
4. **Hotel Logo**

---

## Step 3 — Legal & Compliance

Collect all required legal and regulatory documentation for the property.

1. **GST Registration Certificate**
2. **PAN Card Photo**
3. **Shop Establishment Licence Photo**
4. **Fire Safety NOC**

---

## Stepper Structure

The page should follow this progression:

**Personal Details → Hotel Details → Legal & Compliance**

Each step should:

* Clearly indicate the current step and completed steps.
* Allow users to move to the next step only after required information is completed.
* Allow users to go back and edit previously entered information.
* Clearly distinguish required and optional fields.
* Provide appropriate validation and error messages.
* Support document/image uploads where required.
* Preserve entered information when navigating between steps.
* Provide a final review before the hotel provisioning process is submitted.

The overall interface should feel **professional, clean, modern, and enterprise-level**, suitable for an administrative hotel management system.
